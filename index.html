About me

Hello, I’m Javeria.

Clinical Psychologist based in Lahore, with a decade of counselling experience.

Since 2018, working as a Psychologist with the Punjab AIDS Control Program, supporting people through stigma, difficult diagnoses and life changes.

Questions

Before you begin

Answers to the questions people ask most before their first session.

What happens in the first session?

We talk about what brings you to therapy, a little about your background, and what you would like to change. You share at your own pace, with no pressure.

Does online therapy really work?

Research shows online therapy can be as effective as in-person sessions for many concerns, including anxiety and depression. Many people also find it easier to open up from home.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your goals. Some people notice a difference within a few sessions, while others prefer longer-term support. We review progress together and decide.

Which languages are sessions in?

Sessions are conducted in Urdu.

What if I am in crisis?

This service is not for emergencies. If you are in immediate danger or thinking of harming yourself, please contact emergency services right away.

How do I book a session?

Send a message on WhatsApp or email using the details in the Book a session section, and a time will be arranged with you.
